# v1.1.2: Resizable window

The main window was stuck at one size, which was a problem on smaller or lower-resolution screens. Now you can make it whatever size you like.

## What's new
- **Drag any edge or corner** to resize the window, down to 800 × 420.
- **It never opens taller than your screen.** On laptops and 768p or 900p screens, nothing gets cut off at the bottom anymore.
- **Scrolls when it doesn't fit.** If the window is smaller than the page, scroll to see the rest, with the same thin dark scrollbar as the games list.

## Updating
Download `ChestCounter.zip` below, quit Chest Counter from the tray, replace `ChestCounter.exe` and start it again. Your stats, games and settings carry over.
