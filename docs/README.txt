CHEST COUNTER
=============

A small counter that sits in the top-left of your screen, on top of Fortnite.
Press your button every time you open a chest.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games. Fortnite is a trademark of Epic Games, Inc.

SETUP
1. Unzip and double-click ChestCounter.exe.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" > "Run anyway".
   (It shows this because the app isn't code-signed.)
2. The first time it opens, pick the button you'll press when you open a chest
   and the button for when a match ends. Keys, mouse buttons and controller
   buttons all work.
3. In Fortnite: Settings > Display > Window Mode > "Windowed Fullscreen".
   The counter can't show on top of plain "Fullscreen".
4. Click "Hide to tray". The app keeps running; the chest icon by the clock
   opens it again.

CONTROLLERS
- Xbox controllers work directly. PlayStation and other controllers work if
  they go through Steam Input or DS4Windows.
- The button still does its normal thing in-game, so pick one you rarely use
  (a D-pad direction is a good choice).

PER-GAME STATS
- Press your End game button when a match ends. That game's chests are saved
  and the counter goes back to 0.
- The counter shows which game you're on, your average chests per game, your
  best game and your total. "Undo last save" fixes an accidental End game.

PLAYING WITH A FRIEND (optional)
- In the Friends section, turn on "Share with friends". You get a room code.
- Click Copy and send it to your friend. They paste it into their Room code
  box and turn sharing on too. Your counters then show on each other's screen.
- Stats go through a free public relay server (broker.emqx.io). Only your
  name and chest stats are sent. Anyone with your room code could see them,
  so don't post it publicly.

SEASON ODDS
- Click the "Season odds" tab. Click "Get a free key", log in at
  dash.fortnite-api.com, copy your key and paste it in. Type your Epic name and
  click Refresh stats.
- It shows this season's matches and wins, estimates your chests this season
  (matches x your average chests per game) and your chance of getting each
  loot item. "Got it" marks items you've received.
- Weekly items get a fresh chance each week. Press "New week" when the week
  resets: weekly items then count only the chests you open from that point,
  and their "Got it" switches clear. Season items (like Pirate Shop Access)
  keep counting the whole season.
- Automatic End game (off by default, on the Season odds tab): saves each game
  for you when Fortnite records the match, about a minute or two after it
  ends. Chests you open in the next game stay in the next game. Your End game
  button still works.
- If it says your stats are private: in Fortnite, Settings > Account and
  Privacy > turn on "Show on Career Leaderboard".

NOTES
- The app never touches Fortnite. It only listens for your button presses,
  the same way Discord push-to-talk does.
- By default the counter only shows while Fortnite is open (or while the Chest
  Counter window is open). Turn this off in the Overlay section.
- Holding a button counts once, not over and over. Left and right click can't
  be used (you shoot and aim with them). Avoid E: it also opens doors.
- Everything is saved after every press.
- To quit: right-click the chest icon in the tray > Quit.
